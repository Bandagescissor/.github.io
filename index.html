Chanta - Collaborative Karaoke Made Easy

Turn any gathering into an epic karaoke party! Chanta lets you and your friends create shared music sessions, search for songs together, and build the perfect karaoke playlist collaboratively.

HOW IT WORKS:

  • Host creates a session and shares a simple join code
  • Friends join instantly with the code
  • Everyone searches YouTube for their favorite karaoke songs
  • Add songs to the shared queue together
  • Vote to show your favorites and help the group decide
  • Sync with YouTube playlists for seamless playback

PERFECT FOR:

  • House parties and gatherings
  • Family karaoke nights
  • Friend hangouts
  • Group celebrations
  • Any group that loves singing together

KEY FEATURES:

  • YouTube Integration – Access millions of karaoke tracks
  • Real-time Collaboration – Everyone controls the queue
  • Song Voting – Show your favorites and see what the group likes
  • Simple Join Codes – No complicated setup
  • Live Sync – Changes appear instantly for everyone
  • Built for Groups – The more, the merrier!

WHY CHANTA?

No more fighting over the phone to pick songs. No more one person controlling the music all night. With Chanta, everyone gets a voice. Create a democratic karaoke experience where the whole group participates.

Perfect for karaoke lovers everywhere!

REQUIREMENTS:

  • Android device
  • Internet connection
  • Google account
  • Love for singing (optional but recommended!)
