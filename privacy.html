Privacy Policy for Chanta

Last Updated: 2025

1. Introduction

Chanta is a collaborative karaoke application that allows users to create shared music sessions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using the app.

2. Data We Collect

  • Firebase Firestore: Session data, queue, and analytics
  • Device Storage: Minimal app preferences and cache

2.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Firebase Security Rules protecting database access
  • User authentication required for sensitive operations
  • Regular security updates

2.2 Data Retention

  • Active Sessions: Retained while the session is active
  • Completed Sessions: May be retained for up to 90 days
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized and retained for up to 90 days
  • Search Cache: Automatically expires after 30 days
  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing purposes
  • Share your YouTube credentials with third parties

3.2 We MAY Share Data:

  • With Session Participants: Your display name and participation status are visible to other session members
  • With Service Providers: Firebase / Google Cloud services that help operate the app
  • For Legal Reasons: If required by law or to protect our rights

4. Your Privacy Rights

4.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Export: Request a portable copy of your data
  • Revoke Permissions: Disconnect YouTube access at any time

5. Children’s Privacy

Chanta is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating this page and the "Last Updated" date.
